##### Experience 1: Paper Airplane Making Instruction Guide (PAMIGA) ⌯⌲
Friday, October 2nd - Friday, October 23rd


I was tasked with designing steps to build a viable paper airplane, drawing inspiration from the class activity on paper airplane making, where we watched <i>John Collins'</i> demonstration to fold a “world-record-setting” paper airplane.

I appreciate the professor allowing full creative freedom in designing the instruction guide; I enjoyed the creative aspects, such as creating a timeline journey to show users their progress and the structure of the assignment: developing and writing the instructions.

My favourite memory was adding extra content that would be considered “going above and beyond,” such as the timeline journey; coming up with acronym tips, <i>the FAW (Fingers, Arm, Wrist)</i>, for <i>“How To Fly A Paper Airplane”</i>; and finally getting the assignment done and submitted. However, overcomplicating the assignment and overthinking the instructions was unenjoyable, as energy and time were wasted.
