##### Experience 5: Living Life (Life's Good). 😎🤙
April 8th, 2006 - PRESENT | Level: TBD

I am alive; I was born in April 2006 in Hunan, China; I am currently living life.

I am fortunate and lucky to be alive; I have amazing memories and many awesome adventures; I have come so far and grown up so much; I have experienced many seasons changing and made many friends; some came and went, some stayed a little longer; I am blessed with two parents who love me unconditionally.  

I miss my childhood very much; I am a sentimental soul who yearns for nostalgia, where I wish I could turn back time to a point in life where my innocence and myself never grew up; from chasing and catching fireflies at night, drawing on the sidewalk with chalk, feeding my pet fishies, running up the hill in our backyard, smelling the fresh air, and watching sunsets, excited that I get to live another day. 

It’s not like I don’t enjoy life; everything just became too realistic too quickly: COVID-19, graduating, high school, prom, politics; beliefs, opinions, values; responsibilities. Nevertheless, I am who I am, and I am living who I am and want to become; life is all about living fully.
